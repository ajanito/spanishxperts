Hi, I’m Adán

“Teaching isn’t what I do. It’s who I am.”

I grew up in Mexico in a family of teachers. I learned English the hard way as an adult, at work, with no time to waste, so I know what it feels like to freeze when someone actually talks to you.

In 2012, I moved into teaching Spanish as a second language at Spanish Language Center. Background in literature from Universidad de Guanajuato and a degree in computer systems engineering — two fields that shape everything I do.

That’s what you get: structure first, real conversation next, using today’s tools from streaming to AI. No guesswork.
