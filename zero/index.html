Who it’s for

Built for true beginners.

“Beginner” can mean almost anything. In most classes, one person took three years of high-school Spanish and another knows a few travel phrases. Level Zero is a group designed for people beginning from zero, or very close to it.

  • 0

    You’re starting from scratch

    No Spanish yet, or just a handful of words. You won’t walk into a room where everyone else is already ahead.

  • You want a clear path

    Structure first, real conversation next. You always know what you’re learning and why.

  • You’d rather learn with others

    Hear other learners, learn from their questions, and keep each other going. Shared momentum makes it easier to show up.

How it works

Learn it. Practice it. Use it.

  1. Start together

    The group begins at zero and moves through the same foundational material as a cohort.

  2. Understand the structure

    Clear explanations of how Spanish works, without drowning you in grammar terminology.

  3. Practice it out loud

    Repetition with a purpose: you change the subject, the action, make it negative, ask a question, until the pattern feels familiar.

  4. Use it in conversation

    Speak from early on, with feedback and correction, so good habits stick instead of shortcuts.

“Controlled practice is your heavy bag. Real conversation is your sparring.”Adán J. Ayala, How to Learn Spanish With Streaming

What you’ll learn

A foundation you can actually use.

Not a collection of memorized phrases: patterns that generate hundreds of sentences.

  • Pronunciation and the sounds of Spanish, and how written and spoken Spanish connect.
  • How a sentence is built: subjects, verbs, objects, questions and negatives.
  • Everyday structures such as ser / estar, tener que, ir a, querer and poder + infinitive, and gustar.
  • The present tense to talk about your life, routines, likes and needs.
